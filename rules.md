# Rules

Fixed constraints that apply to every generated video, regardless of theme
or character.

- **Duration:** 30-35 seconds
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16
- **Max scenes:** 5 scenes per video
- **Captions:** must be burned into the video (not relying on platform
  auto-captions)
- **Content restrictions:**
  - No political content
  - No religious content
  - No copyrighted music (use licensed/royalty-free audio only)
