# Hulku — Recurring Character

Source: extracted from a shared ChatGPT conversation
(https://chatgpt.com/share/6a7dabd0-0510-83ea-954d-6874fc29a6c3), "Hulku
Master Prompt". This is a **recurring** character — unlike the pipeline's
normal one-off-character-per-run default (see `CLAUDE.md`'s Input
section), Hulku's design, voice, and personality must stay identical
across every video that uses him.

## Trigger

If the sheet row's **Video Title** or **Description** mentions "Hulku"
(case-insensitive), use this file as the character's identity reference
instead of inventing a new character. Everything else (the specific
story/plot, other one-off characters, locations, dialogue) is still
invented fresh per run — only Hulku's own design/voice/personality is
locked.

## Identity

Completely original cartoon character — **not** Marvel Hulk, not a copy of
any existing Hulk design. A small, muscular, green humanoid cartoon
character with an adorable human-child-like face.

## Age / appearance

Child-like facial appearance with cute, innocent facial proportions, but a
muscular and powerful body. Must NOT look like an actual human child with
adult muscles grafted on — he's a stylized fictional cartoon creature, not
a realistic human.

## Body

- Short, compact body (~110 cm tall)
- Extremely muscular physique: broad shoulders, large chest, thick arms,
  powerful forearms, strong legs, compact muscular torso
- Exaggerated strength without grotesque anatomy
- Green skin — smooth, stylized cartoon skin material, matte cinematic
  look, subtle realistic surface texture, soft highlights
- No glowing skin, no metallic skin, no plastic-toy appearance
- No body mutation between shots — body stays identical scene to scene

## Face

- Face skin is the **same green** as the body — no skin-tone/color change
  between face and body. The face is human-child-*shaped* (proportions,
  cuteness), not human-*colored*; never render Hulku with realistic human
  skin on the face while the body stays green.
- Cute, human-child-inspired facial structure
- Large but proportionally believable expressive eyes (dark brown,
  natural catchlights — cute but not anime; no glowing eyes, no oversized
  anime eyes)
- Rounded cheeks, small rounded nose, soft child-like jawline
- Slightly oversized head relative to body (cute character appeal)
- Strong expressive eyebrows, highly readable expressions
- Very short dark black hair, simple consistent hairstyle, same hairline
  every time

## Hands

Four to five simplified cartoon fingers (whichever the generation model
naturally produces), always anatomically consistent — no mutation, no
extra fingers, no human-child hand appearance.

## Voice (immutable — never vary)

Young North Indian male voice, slightly deep for his small body: warm,
innocent, energetic, slightly rough, comedic timing, emotionally
expressive. Same pitch, vocal identity, accent, speaking rhythm, tone,
pronunciation, and emotional character across every scene/video. Never a
robotic voice, never an adult heroic "monster" voice, never an exaggerated
cartoon voice, never a different voice actor.

## Personality

Innocent, funny, determined, kind-hearted. Slightly short-tempered when
insulted, emotionally sensitive, but never cruel. Strong but humble.

## Wardrobe

Clothing may change only because the story's location/activity changes —
Hulku's body must never change when clothing changes. Pick an outfit
appropriate to the run's story (examples from the source material: a
tight sleeveless red training vest + black athletic shorts + worn training
shoes for a gym setting; an orange construction safety vest + dark blue
work trousers + small protective helmet + heavy-duty work shoes for a
labor setting). Keep the same outfit consistent across all scenes within
one video unless the story has a believable scene transition that
justifies a change.

## Story pattern (only when the sheet doesn't specify its own plot)

If the sheet's Title/Description just says to use Hulku without dictating
a specific plot, default to this emotional arc — but invent a **fresh**
specific scenario, insult, and struggle each time; never reuse the exact
same dialogue/insults/success path from a previous run:

1. **Beginning** — Insult & humiliation. Someone mocks Hulku (about his
   size, strength, or worth). Tone should feel harsh, mocking, and
   genuinely humiliating; dialogue should feel raw and painful, not
   cartoonish.
2. **Turning point** — Self-decision. Instead of fighting back
   immediately, Hulku quietly decides to prove himself through action/work
   rather than confrontation.
3. **Struggle phase** — He works hard; his small body keeps getting into
   situations that require enormous strength, which can be genuinely
   funny.
4. **Success & growth** — Final confrontation & resolution: Hulku
   encounters the same person who insulted him (often via an act of
   genuine courage, e.g. saving them), the bully apologizes, and Hulku
   refuses revenge — explicitly rejecting the idea that strength is about
   humiliating weaker people. Ending should feel emotionally satisfying
   and powerful, establishing Hulku as physically powerful but
   emotionally kind.

Every dialogue line should end with the Hindi filler "re" (colloquial
tone), matching the source material's Hinglish dialogue style, if the
run's dialogue is in Hindi/Hinglish. Don't force this if the sheet's
idea is clearly meant to be in a different language/style.
